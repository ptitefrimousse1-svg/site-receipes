💡 Summer Food Tips

  • Stay Hydrated: Use cucumber and watermelon for extra hydration
  • Fresh & Raw: Enjoy vegetables raw in salads to preserve nutrients
  • Preserve: Make jams and freeze berries for winter months
  • Light Meals: Opt for cold soups and salads during hot days
  • Local Markets: Buy directly from farmers for the freshest produce
