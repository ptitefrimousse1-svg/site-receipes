Snicker bars

⏱️ Prep Time
20 min
👥 Servings
16 slices

Ingredients

  • Chocolate
  • Raw Cashews
  • Pitted Medjool Dates
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Peanut Butter
  • Maple Syrup
  • Chopped Peanuts

Instructions

  1. Line a baking dish with parchment paper.
  2. Add raw cashews and dates to the food processor and pulse until crumbly and sticky. Press into the dish in an even layer. Freeze for 5 minutes.
  3. Melt together the peanut butter, the maple syrup and vanilla extract then pour the mixture over the nougat layer. Sprinkle with chopped peanuts into an even layer. Freeze for 5 minutes.
  4. Melt the chocolate, then cover the peanut and caramel layer with it. Freeze one last time for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
  5. Cut into slices, then sprinkle with sea salt. Remove the slices from the baking pan and serve! Enjoy!
