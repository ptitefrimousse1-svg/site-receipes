Cauliflower gratin and salad

⏱️ Prep Time
15 min
🔥 Cook Time
25-30 min
👥 Servings
6

Ingredients

  • 1 cauliflower (about 1.8 lb)
  • Some grated cheese
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Fill a large saucepan with salted water and bring to a boil. Add the cauliflower florets to the boiling water. Cook for about 5 to 7 minutes, until they are slightly tender but still crunchy. This partial cooking helps to preserve the cauliflower's texture after baking. Drain the florets well and set aside.
  2. Prepare the béchamel sauce: In a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the flour and mix well to form a roux. Cook for about 1 to 2 minutes, stirring constantly with a wooden spatula or whisk, until the mixture turns slightly golden.Add the milk: Slowly pour in the milk while continuing to stir. Allow the sauce to thicken, stirring regularly. The béchamel should reach a creamy, thick consistency, which takes about 5 minutes. Season the sauce.
  3. Prepare the dish: Arrange the cauliflower florets in the prepared dish, spreading them evenly.
  4. Add the sauce: Pour the béchamel sauce over the cauliflower and add some grated cheese on top.
  5. Bake the gratin: Place the dish in the preheated oven. Bake the gratin for 25 to 30 minutes.
  6. Serve it with a salad and enjoy!
💡 Chef's Tips

To make it tastier you can add nutmeg nut in your béchamel!
