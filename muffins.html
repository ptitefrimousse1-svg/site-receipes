Blueberries muffins

⏱️ Prep Time
10 min
🔥 Cook Time
15-20 min
👥 Servings
10 muffins

Ingredients

  • 2 mashed banana
  • 2 eggs
  • 60ml peanut butter
  • 80g oat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 240g bluberries (fresh or frozen)

Instructions

  1. Mix the wet ingredients together
  2. Add the dry ingredients and mix until combined.
  3. Fold in the blueberries.
  4. Spoon to muffin pan lined with muffin liners.
  5. Bake at 180°C for about 15-20min.
  6. Enjoy!
