💡 Winter Food Tips

  • Boost Immunity: Focus on vitamin C rich foods like oranges and broccoli
  • Slow Cooking: Winter is perfect for slow-cooked stews and soups
  • Root Vegetables: Store root vegetables in a cool cellar for months
  • Warm Spices: Use ginger, cinnamon, and nutmeg for extra warmth
  • Meal Prep: Make large batches and freeze for easy winter meals
