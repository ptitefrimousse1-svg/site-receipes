Red berries crumble

⏱️ Prep Time
15 min
🔥 Cook Time
25-30 min
👥 Servings
4 or 5

Ingredients

  • 100g flour
  • 50g oat
  • 80g sugar
  • 90g butter
  • 120g red berries
  • 1 tablespoon sugar

Instructions

  1. Add the red berries in a baking dish.
  2. Put the flour, oats and sugar in a bowl. Add the softened butter, cut into cubes, and rub together with your fingertips until you have a crumbly mixture.
  3. Sprinkle the crumble mixture evenly over the fruit and bake for 25-30 minutes at 190°C.
  4. Enjoy!
