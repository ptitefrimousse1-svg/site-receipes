Summer salad

⏱️ Prep Time
15 min
🔥 Cook Time
30 min
👥 Servings
6

Ingredients

  • 400g puy lentils (green lentils will do)
  • 1tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1.5 tsp dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • handful flat leaf parsley, chopped
  • salt + pepper
  • cherry tomatoes
  • 1 courgette, sliced into thin rounds
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 mozzarella

Instructions

  1. Cover the lentils with plenty of water in a large saucepan with a lid and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil then simmer until the lentils are al dente, about 25-35 minutes, depending on your lentils.
  2. In a large salad bowl, mix together the red wine vinegar, mustard, oregano, red onion, olive oil, parsley and a pinch of salt + pepper. Add in the sliced tomatoes.
  3. Slice the courgette thinly, and fry on a medium low heat in a small amount of oil until golden, adding the garlic just before it is cooked for 2-3 mins. Add to the salad bowl with the dressing.
  4. Slice the mozzarella and add it to the salad bowl.
  5. Drain the lentils and add to the salad bowl with the chopped basil.
  6. Serve. It is best served warm, but is also good at room temperature.
